Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Matching, Puzzles, and Drawing work completely offline. Storytime works offline too using built-in story templates. An internet connection enables AI-generated stories and premium voice narration.

Is the app really free?

Yes. Kidz Korner is completely free with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Are the stories safe for my child?

Yes. All story inputs are filtered for inappropriate content. The AI is configured with strict safety guidelines to generate only gentle, wholesome stories appropriate for ages 3-6. Generated stories are also scanned before being displayed.

Does the app collect any data?

No. All data (photos, drawings, character names) stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded or tracked. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What ages is this app for?

Kidz Korner is designed for children ages 3-6, with large touch targets, colorful visuals, and simple navigation that young children can use independently.

Can I create my own content?

Yes! You can create custom matching card decks with your own photos, add your own puzzle images, create story characters with selfies, and save drawings to a gallery.
