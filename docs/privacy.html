Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Kidz Korner is designed with children's privacy as a top priority. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and what is never collected.

Data Collection

Kidz Korner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. We do not use analytics, advertising frameworks, or tracking of any kind.

Photos and Camera

When you choose to add photos for matching cards, puzzles, or story characters, images are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to any server.

Player Name

The name entered on first launch is stored only on your device using local storage. It is used to personalize the home screen and is never transmitted anywhere.

AI Story Generation

When connected to the internet, story prompts (hero name, animal, place, food, color, and silly sound) are sent to an AI service to generate personalized stories. No personal information beyond these story inputs is transmitted. The AI service does not retain this data after generating the story.

Voice Narration

Story text may be sent to a text-to-speech service for narration. No personal information is included in these requests. The service does not retain any data after generating the audio.

Content Safety

All story inputs are filtered for inappropriate content before being sent to the AI service. Generated stories are scanned for age-appropriateness before being displayed. The AI is configured with strict safety guidelines to produce only gentle, wholesome content suitable for children ages 3-6.

API Keys

If a user optionally enters their own API key in Settings, it is stored securely in the device's Keychain and is only transmitted to the respective API provider for authentication.

Third-Party Services

Kidz Korner uses the following third-party services for story features:

  • Anthropic (Claude) for story text generation — Privacy Policy
  • ElevenLabs for voice narration — Privacy Policy

Both services process only the story content, not personal data. These services are only contacted when an internet connection is available and the Storytime feature is used.

On-Device Data

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Player name
  • Custom matching card decks and photos
  • Custom puzzle images
  • Story character names and photos
  • Saved drawings

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children and complies with applicable children's privacy regulations, including COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users. The app contains no advertising, no social features, and no account creation.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
